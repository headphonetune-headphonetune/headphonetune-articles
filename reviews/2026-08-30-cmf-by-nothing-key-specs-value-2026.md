Weight, and Fit The headline number here's 5.92 grams per earbud. That is genuinely light. The spec sheet mentions a "3-Point Clip Design" and a "Titanium Wire Core." What that means practically is that the clip is meant to flex open to fit your ear and then snap back to its original shape. The pressure is spread across three contact points rather than one pinch point. That's a smart approach; a single-point clip can get sore after A hour. The three-point distribution suggests the fit is more stable and less fatiguing. Which matters if you're wearing these for a full workday or a long hike. The enclosure is a mix of PC/ABS plastic and TPU (a rubbery material) for the clip, with an aluminum alloy in the case. The TPU on the clip is the key detail here-it's the part that touches your skin, and it gives a bit of grip so the bud doesn't slide off when you sweat. The "Open Ear" placement means nothing goes into your ear canal. That's the core trade-off of this entire product category, and we'll get to that in the performance section.
