### Frequently Asked Questions About the MAONO MH601 Wired
### Does the MH601 have a built-in microphone?
No, it has no built-in mic, so you can't use it for calls or voice commands. You'll need a separate microphone for voice communication.

### Can I use these headphones with my phone or laptop?
Yes, they work with devices that have a 3.5mm jack, including smartphones, PCs, Macs - and laptops. For newer iPhones without a 3.5mm port, you'll need a Lightning or USB-C to 3.5mm adapter, which isn't included.

### What cables come in the box?
You get one detachable 9.8-foot spring cable with 3.5mm to 6.35mm connectors and an additional interchangeable 6.35mm jack. This lets you plug into mixers, audio interfaces, or other gear with a 6.35mm input.

### How much noise do these headphones block out?
The closed-back design with protein earmuffs blocks about 85% of ambient noise. That's passive isolation. Not active noise cancellation, so it reduces background sound but won't silence loud environments completely.

### Are they comfortable for long recording sessions?
The headband uses protein memory cushions and the earcups swivel 90°, which helps with fit and single-ear monitoring. The listing claims they're engineered for 8+ hours of fatigue-free use, though individual comfort will vary.
