### Frequently Asked Questions About the BERIBES Bluetooth Headphones
### How long does the battery actually last. And how long does it take to charge?
The 500mAh battery is rated for more than 65 hours of playback and over 950 hours of standby on a single charge. A full charge takes about 2.5 hours, though the description also mentions 1.5 to 2 hours.

### Can I use these headphones with a wired connection if the battery dies?
Yes. A 3.5mm audio cable is included. So you can switch to wired mode when the power runs out.

### Can I connect these to two devices at the same time?
Yes. Two smart devices can be paired simultaneously, so you can take a call from your phone while watching something on a tablet without re-pairing.

### How much do these headphones weigh, and do they fold up?
They weigh 0.38lb (6.08 ounces) and the earcups fold for storage in a bag or suitcase. The adjustable headband is meant to fit a range of head sizes.

### Are these headphones water resistant?
No. The water resistance level is listed as not water resistant, so keep them away from rain and sweat-heavy workouts.
