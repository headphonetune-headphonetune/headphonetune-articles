# Crusher 720 2026: 5 Must-Have Accessories You’ll Actually Use

### Why Your Crusher 720 Deserves More Than Just the Box

The Skullcandy Crusher 720 delivers that signature haptic bass that rattles your skull in the best way possible. But if you’re only using what came in the box, you’re leaving performance on the table. A few smart additions can make your listening experience noticeably cleaner, more comfortable, and more versatile. Here’s what I’d grab first.

### 1. A Proper USB-C Braided Cable

The stock charging cable works, but it’s usually thin and prone to tangling. A braided USB-C cable is a cheap upgrade that actually matters. You’ll get better durability, less tangling, and a cable that won’t fray after a few months in a backpack. Look for one with a right-angle connector if you tend to listen while charging — it keeps the cable out of your way and reduces strain on the port. If you’re curious about how cable quality affects other headphones, the [Oraolo Open Ear AC225 specs and performance review](https://www.headphonetune.com/review/oraolo-open-ear-ac225-specs-performance-review/) touches on connection stability and build considerations that apply here too.

### 2. Memory Foam Earpads

The stock earpads on the Crusher 720 are decent, but they compress over time. Memory foam replacements are the single biggest comfort upgrade you can make. They conform to your ears better, distribute pressure more evenly, and often improve passive isolation. That matters because the Crusher’s haptic bass driver works by vibrating the whole ear cup — if the seal is loose, you lose low-end impact. Swapping pads takes about two minutes and makes long listening sessions far more enjoyable. Just make sure you buy pads specifically sized for the Crusher 720; generic “universal” pads often don’t fit the mounting ring properly.

### 3. A Bluetooth Transmitter for TV or Gaming

The Crusher 720 is primarily a mobile headphone, but its low-latency codec support means it can double as a TV headset if you have the right gear. A dedicated Bluetooth transmitter with aptX Low Latency support plugs into your TV’s optical or AUX output and sends audio with minimal delay. This is a game changer for watching movies or playing games where lip-sync matters. Without it, you’ll notice a slight lag that’s annoying once you spot it. If you’re also considering a more casual everyday headset for calls and podcasts, the [Sarevile Bluetooth headset review](https://www.headphonetune.com/review/sarevile-bluetooth-headset-headphones-review-2026/) covers a lighter option that might pair well with your Crusher for different use cases.

### 4. A Hard-Shell Carrying Case

The Crusher 720 folds flat, but it doesn’t come with a protective case. That’s a real oversight for a headphone with moving hinges and a sensitive bass mechanism. A hard-shell case with a molded interior keeps the hinges from getting knocked around and protects the ear cups from scratches. You don’t need a massive case — a compact one that fits the folded shape works fine. If you travel or throw your headphones into a gym bag, this is non-negotiable. The cost is small compared to the cost of replacing a broken hinge.

### 5. A Headphone Stand or Wall Mount

This one sounds obvious, but hear me out. The Crusher 720’s headband padding is soft foam, and leaving it on a desk face-down flattens that padding unevenly. A simple stand keeps the headband in its natural shape and prevents pressure marks. A wall mount is even better if you’re short on desk space — it also keeps the headphones visible and easy to grab. If you’re into the full accessory ecosystem for your setup, the [5 essential accessories for your Skullcandy Crusher 720](https://www.headphonetune.com/review/5-essential-accessories-for-your-skullcandy-crusher-720-2026/) guide rounds out the list with a few more ideas you might not have considered.

### Final Thoughts

None of these accessories are flashy, but they all address real weaknesses in the Crusher 720 experience. Start with the earpads and the cable — those are the cheapest and most impactful upgrades. The transmitter and case matter more if you use the headphones beyond your phone. And the stand is just good hygiene for your gear. Pick what fits your habits, and your Crusher will feel like a much more complete product.
