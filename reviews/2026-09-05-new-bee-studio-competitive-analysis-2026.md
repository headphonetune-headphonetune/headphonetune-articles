### Frequently Asked Questions About the New Bee Studio
### What devices can I connect these headphones to without needing an adapter?
The headphones come with both 3.5mm and 6.35mm gold-plated plugs built in, so you can plug directly into laptops, desktops, digital pianos, guitar amps - and audio interfaces without a separate adapter.

### How heavy are these headphones, and what materials are used for comfort?
They weigh 210g, which is lighter than the typical 270g studio monitor. The ear cups are made with skin-friendly protein leather and memory foam, and the headband is adjustable, so they're designed for long sessions without fatigue.

 We took a close look at what the New Bee Studio offers and how it compares to other options in its class.### Can I use these for single-ear monitoring while DJing or recording?
Yes, the ear cups swivel 90 degrees, allowing you to rotate one cup away from your ear for single-side monitoring, which is useful for tracking, live DJ sets, or booth work.

### Do these headphones block out background noise?
They offer passive noise reduction through the over-ear design, which physically seals around your ears to dampen ambient sound. They don't have active noise cancellation. So loud, constant noises may still bleed through.

The frequency range is 10 Hz to 40,000 Hz, which extends beyond the typical 20 Hz-20 kHz human hearing range. This extra headroom can help you hear very low sub-bass and very high treble details when mixing or mastering, though most consumer audio won't use the full range.
