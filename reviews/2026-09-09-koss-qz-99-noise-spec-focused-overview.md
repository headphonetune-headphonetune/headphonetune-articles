### Frequently Asked Questions About the Koss QZ-99 Noise
### How does this headphone reduce noise if it's not active noise cancellation?
It uses passive noise reduction, meaning the Pneumalite ear cushions and secure headband physically block ambient sound rather than generating anti-noise signals. This works best in steady-noise environments like scanners or metal-detecting use. Not for canceling unpredictable sounds like conversations.

### Can I use this with a racing scanner or metal detector that only has a mono output?
Yes. The stereo/mono switch lets you adapt the headphone to mono sources, so you'll hear the signal properly even if the device outputs in mono. This is a key reason it's marketed for those uses.

You get a standard 3.5mm jack and a wired connection, so it works with laptops, desktops, tablets, cellphones, and music production equipment that have a headphone port. The 8-foot coiled cord gives you extra reach without tangling easily.

### Is this headphone comfortable for long listening sessions?
The on-ear design uses Pneumalite ear cushions and an extra-secure headband for passive isolation. Which suggests a snug fit. However, the data doesn't specify clamping force or cushion softness, so comfort over hours will depend on your head size and tolerance for on-ear pressure.

### Does the volume control on the earcup work with any device?
The volume control is wired into the headphone cable, so it adjusts the signal level before it reaches the drivers. It should work with any device that has a standard 3.5mm output, but it won't control volume on devices that use digital volume only, like some phones with USB-C audio adapters.
