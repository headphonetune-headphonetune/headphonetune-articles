What the amplification specs actually mean The amplifier type is solid state, which is the standard for this category. You're not getting tubes. ### Frequently Asked Questions About the Mackie HM Series
### How many people can listen at the same time with this?
Up to four. It takes one stereo source and splits it to four headphone outputs. Each with its own level control.
