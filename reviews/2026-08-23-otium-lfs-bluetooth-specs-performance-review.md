### Frequently Asked Questions About the Otium LFS Bluetooth
### How long does the battery last on a full charge?
Up to 8 hours of music playback per full charge, and it takes about 1 hour to recharge. The battery status shows on iOS screens. And a low-battery voice notification alerts you.

### Can I use these earbuds for intense workouts without worrying about sweat or water damage?
Yes. They've an IPX7 waterproof rating, meaning they can handle heavy sweat and even brief submersion in water. The sealed shell and nano-coating protect the internals during running, cycling, or gym sessions.

### What is the Bluetooth range and can I connect them to multiple devices?
The Bluetooth range is up to 30 feet in open spaces and about 10 feet with obstacles. You can pair them with two devices at the same time. And they work with Android, iOS, and other Bluetooth devices.

### Do the earbuds have controls for music and calls without touching my phone?
Yes. There are easy-to-use buttons for skip. Play, and pause, plus call handling. The built-in mic also gives voice notifications for incoming calls. So you don't need to reach for your phone during exercise.

### What eartip sizes are included for a secure fit?
The package includes four additional pairs of eartips in sizes XS, S, M - and L, along with the ones pre-installed. The ear-hook design and silicone material help keep them stable during movement.
