### Frequently Asked Questions About the CMF BY Nothing
### How long does the battery last with and without noise cancellation?
You get up to 100 hours of playback with ANC off, and 50 hours with ANC on. A 5-minute charge provides 4 hours of listening.

### Can I use these headphones with a wired connection?
Yes. They have a 3.5mm jack and come with a cable, so you can use them via AUX with devices that lack Bluetooth.

### What devices are these headphones compatible with?
They work with iOS. Android, macOS, and Windows, plus cellphones, gaming consoles, laptops, tablets, and TVs. Google Fast Pair and Microsoft Swift Pair make setup quick on supported devices.

### How does the noise cancellation work?
You get up to 40 dB of Adaptive Active Noise Cancellation with a hybrid system. You can press the ANC button to switch to Transparency Mode when you need to hear surroundings.

### Can I adjust the sound profile?
Yes. The Nothing X app lets you customize EQ, use a Bass & Treble slider, and set up a Personal Sound Profile. It also includes CMF Spatial Audio with cinema and concert modes.
