### Frequently Asked Questions About the YINYOO KZ ZS10
### What type of drivers do these earphones use. And what does that mean for sound quality?
They use a hybrid setup with 4 balanced armature drivers and 1 dynamic driver (4BA+1DD). This combination is designed to deliver deep bass, clear mids, and detailed highs, with a balanced sound signature across the frequency range of 7-40000 Hz.

### Can I use these with my phone or gaming console?
Yes. They connect via a standard 3.5 mm jack, so they work with any device that has that port, including phones, laptops, and gaming consoles. The cable is detachable, and you can swap it for other compatible IEM cables if needed.
