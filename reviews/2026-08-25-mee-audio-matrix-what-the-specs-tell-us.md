### Frequently Asked Questions About the MEE audio Matrix
### How does the CinemaEAR feature help when watching movies?
CinemaEAR provides several sound enhancement modes, including two Clear Voice modes that boost speech frequencies for better dialogue clarity, even at lower volumes. You can switch between these modes at the touch of a button.

### Will these headphones work with my TV or phone if they don't support aptX Low Latency?
Yes, they use standard Bluetooth 4.2 and also include a detachable 3.5mm audio cable for wired use with devices that lack Bluetooth, such as in-flight entertainment systems. The low-latency benefit only applies when the connected device supports aptX Low Latency.

### How long does the battery last and how long does it take to charge?
Battery life is up to 30 hours of talk time and 28 hours of media playback, with a full charge taking about 2 hours. The notification lights can be disabled to save power during low-light viewing.

### Are these headphones comfortable for long viewing sessions?
They have ultra-soft memory foam earpads and an over-ear design, though the ear placement is listed as 'On Ear,' so fit may vary depending on ear size. They fold flat for storage when not in use.

### Can I take calls with these headphones?
Yes. They include a built-in microphone and integrated controls for taking calls and managing music playback with phones and tablets. The controls are touch-based.
