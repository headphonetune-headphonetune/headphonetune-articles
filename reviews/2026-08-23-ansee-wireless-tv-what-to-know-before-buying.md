### Frequently Asked Questions About the Ansee Wireless TV
### How do I connect these headphones to my TV?
Plug the included RF transmitter into your TV's Optical. 3.5mm AUX, or RCA audio output, then turn on the headphones. They are pre-paired, so no setup or pairing steps are needed.

### Can I use these headphones if my TV doesn't have Bluetooth?
Yes. They use 2.4GHz RF wireless technology instead of Bluetooth. So they work with any TV that has an Optical, 3.5mm AUX, or RCA audio out port.

### What should I do if I don't hear any sound?
Change your TV's audio output setting to 'External Speakers.' If using an optical cable, make sure the TV audio format is set to 'PCM' or that Dolby/DTS is turned off.

### How long does the battery last - and how long does it take to charge?
The battery lasts about 24 hours on a full charge, which takes roughly 4 hours. If the battery runs out. You can still use them wired via the 3.5mm cable.

### Will the audio stay in sync with the video?
Yes. The RF transmitter is designed for zero latency, so there should be no noticeable delay between what you see and what you hear.
