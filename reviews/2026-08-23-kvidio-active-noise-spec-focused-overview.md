### Frequently Asked Questions About the KVIDIO Active Noise
### How long does the battery last with noise cancelling turned on versus off?
In wireless active noise cancelling mode, you get up to 40 hours of playtime. Switching to standard music mode extends that to 70 hours. And using the included 3.5mm audio cable in wired mode gives you even longer use.

### Can I hear my surroundings while wearing these headphones?
Yes, there's an active transparency mode that lets you hear traffic or talk to people without taking the headphones off. This is separate from the noise cancelling mode, which blocks up to 95% of ambient noise.

### How fast does the quick charging feature work?
A 5-minute charge provides 4 hours of playtime. A full charge takes about 2.5 hours.

### Are these headphones suitable for making phone calls?
Yes, they have a built-in microphone and use digital signal processing (DSP) to deliver clearer voice quality for phone conferences or conversations. They are listed for business and calling use.

### Can I use them wired if the battery dies?
Yes, the package includes a detachable 3.5mm audio cable, so you can plug them in for wired use. they're also foldable and lightweight, making them portable for travel.
