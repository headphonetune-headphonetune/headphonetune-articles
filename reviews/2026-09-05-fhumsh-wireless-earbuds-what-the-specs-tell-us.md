### Frequently Asked Questions About the Fhumsh Wireless Earbuds
### How long does the battery last on a single charge. And what's the total playtime with the case?
Each earbud provides 8 hours of playtime per charge. And with the charging case, you get a total of 50 hours of use. The case itself is IPX7 waterproof.

### Are these earbuds suitable for intense workouts or running in the rain?
Yes, they have an IPX7 waterproof rating and a nano-coating inside to prevent sweat damage. The reinforced earhooks resist vibration up to 8G, so they stay secure during HIIT or cycling.

### Can I connect these to two devices at once. Like a laptop and a phone?
Yes. They support dual-device pairing, so you can switch between a laptop call and phone music seamlessly. They also work with laptops, desktops, smartphones, tablets, gaming consoles, smart speakers, and TVs.

### Do these earbuds have noise cancellation for calls?
They use Environmental Noise Cancellation (ENC) with beamforming mics, which enhances call clarity even in 25mph winds. This is for reducing background noise during calls. Not for blocking out all ambient sound during music playback.

### How do I know the battery level of the case and each earbud?
The charging case has an LED display that shows real-time power levels (0-100%) for both the case and each individual earbud. This lets you check remaining battery at a glance.
