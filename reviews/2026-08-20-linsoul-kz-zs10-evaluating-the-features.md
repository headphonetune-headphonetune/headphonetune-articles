If the crossover isn't handled well, you get a disjointed sound where the bass and treble feel like they're coming from different places. KZ has done a decent job here, but it's not flawless. The transition between the dynamic driver and the balanced armatures is noticeable on certain tracks, especially acoustic recordings where the bass guitar and vocals overlap.
