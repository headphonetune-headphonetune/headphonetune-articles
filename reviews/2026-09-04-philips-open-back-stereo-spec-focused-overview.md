The Open-Back Elephant in the Room The headline feature is the open-back acoustic structure. For everyday listening. The listing says it delivers a "natural, spacious soundstage." That's not marketing fluff - it's physics. Open-back headphones have perforated or mesh-covered ear cups that let air pass through the drivers in both directions. Sound waves escape out the back instead of bouncing around inside a sealed chamber. The result is a wide, airy presentation where instruments feel like they occupy distinct positions in space rather than being crammed together inside your head. For classical. Jazz, acoustic music, and even well-mastered rock, this is genuinely more realistic and less fatiguing to listen to for long periods. Here's the flip side: these leak sound like a sieve. Someone sitting three feet away will hear exactly what you're playing. And at moderate volume it'll be audible across a room. You will also hear everything around you - keyboard clicks, traffic, a partner watching TV. The listing mentions "noise control: None. " which is an understatement.
