### Frequently Asked Questions About the Cowyawn USB C
### Which devices can I use these headphones with?
They work with any device that has a USB-C audio jack, including iPhone 15/16/17 series, Android phones like Google Pixel, laptops, MacBooks, tablets, Chromebooks, and Fire Tablets. They aren't compatible with devices that only have a 3.5mm headphone jack.

 Our team spent some time comparing the Cowyawn USB C against similar products in this space.### Can I use the microphone for gaming or real-time communication?
No. The product notes state these headphones cannot be used for real-time communication while playing games. They are fine for phone calls and voice pickup in general use. But not for live in-game chat.

### Do these headphones need to be charged?
No. They are wired and plug-and-play via USB-C, so they draw power from the connected device and need no charging.

### Are they comfortable for long listening sessions and easy to carry?
The earcups are padded, the headband is cushioned and adjustable, and the earcups swivel 90 degrees. The whole unit folds up and weighs 0.25 kg, making it compact enough to pack or hang on luggage.

### How much ambient noise do they block?
The over-ear design with sound isolation blocks over 90% of ambient sounds. Note this is passive isolation, not active noise cancellation, and they aren't water resistant.
