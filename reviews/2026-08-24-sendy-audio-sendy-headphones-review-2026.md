### Frequently Asked Questions About the SENDY AUDIO SENDY
### What kind of driver does the Egret use - and what size is it?
It uses a planar magnetic driver measuring 98 x 84 mm, with a nano-scale composite diaphragm that has a magnetic cutting layer under 800 nanometers thick for faster response and higher resolution.

 After spending some time with the SENDY AUDIO SENDY, here is what we found.### Is this headphone suitable for use with a phone or laptop directly?
Yes, it has a 24-ohm impedance and 95 dB sensitivity, so it can be driven by phones, laptops, tablets - and other listed devices without a separate amplifier, though a dedicated amp may improve headroom.

### What cables and connectors come with the Egret?
You get a detachable 1.8m triple-composite 4-core cable with a 4.4mm balanced plug. Plus a short 4.4mm-to-3.5mm adapter cable and a 3.5mm-to-6.35mm conversion plug. The headphone itself uses dual 3.5mm mono jacks on each earcup.

### Does the Egret block outside noise?
No. It is an open-type headphone with passive noise cancellation only, meaning it does not isolate sound and will leak audio both ways. It's designed for quiet listening environments. Not commuting or noisy rooms.

### What materials are the earcups and pads made of?
The earcups are North American black walnut, chosen for hardness and density, and the pads combine premium lamb leather with skin-friendly velour. The headphone weighs 420 g and isn't water resistant.
