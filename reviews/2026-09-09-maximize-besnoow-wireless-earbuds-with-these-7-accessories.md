### Pair These Accessories With BESNOOW Wireless Earbuds (2026)

If you’ve just picked up a pair of BESNOOW wireless earbuds, you already know the core sound is solid. But the right accessories can turn a good daily driver into something that actually fits your routine — whether that’s commuting, working out, or just not losing the case in a couch cushion. Here’s what’s worth adding and what you can skip.

### Start With a Better Fit

The stock ear tips that ship with most earbuds, including BESNOOW, tend to be one-size-fits-most — which really means they fit almost no one perfectly. If you feel like you’re constantly pushing the buds back in or the bass sounds thin, the problem is likely a poor seal, not the driver. A set of memory foam tips, like Comply or a generic brand, will expand to match your ear canal and lock the bud in place. The difference is immediate: lower frequencies get punchier, outside noise drops, and you’ll stop fiddling with them every ten minutes.

Foam tips also help if you run or cycle. The BESNOOW’s IP rating handles sweat, but a loose fit will still let them rattle around. Spend a few minutes trying different sizes — most people need one size larger than they think.

### A Charging Case That Does More

The BESNOOW case is fine for pocket duty, but it lacks wireless charging. If you’re tired of plugging in a cable every couple of days, a slim Qi charging pad for your desk or nightstand changes the habit entirely. Just drop the case on it when you get home. No hunting for a USB-C cable, no thinking about battery levels until you grab the buds in the morning.

For travel, consider a small hard-shell case that fits the BESNOOW charging case plus a short cable and a spare set of tips. The original case scuffs easily if you toss it in a backpack with keys or coins. A $10 padded pouch will keep it looking new and stop the lid from popping open unexpectedly.

### Ear Hooks or Wings for Active Use

If you’re using BESNOOW for gym sessions, the standard fit might not survive burpees or heavy bag work. Clip-on ear hooks — the soft silicone kind that wrap around the outer ear — are a cheap fix. They don’t change the sound signature, and they make the buds feel almost locked in. Some people find them uncomfortable after an hour, so try a pair with thin, flexible silicone rather than rigid plastic.

For running outdoors, also grab a reflective armband or a small clip for your shirt. The earbuds themselves are fine, but your phone or player needs to be somewhere accessible. A sweat-resistant armband with a transparent touchscreen window works well and keeps you from stopping to dig through a pocket mid-run.

### Keep Them Clean

Earwax buildup will mute the BESNOOW’s treble faster than any accessory failure. A simple cleaning kit with a soft brush and a magnetic pickup tool is worth having. Wipe the buds with a dry microfiber cloth after each workout, and use the brush on the mesh grille weekly. Avoid using water or alcohol directly on the drivers — that’s the fastest way to ruin the coating.

If you’re also using a dedicated sleep earbud set, you’ll want to keep those separate from your daily pair. Mixing hygiene routines gets messy, and the cleaning needs differ. Check out our guide on whether [Mudtun sleep earbuds are right for your needs](https://www.headphonetune.com/review/mudtun-sleep-earbuds-right-for-your-needs/) if you’re considering a second pair for nighttime use.

### What to Skip

Don’t bother with Bluetooth transmitters or DAC dongles for the BESNOOW — they already support the codecs they need, and adding a dongle just introduces another point of failure. Similarly, skip “premium” replacement cables or ear tip brands that claim to change the soundstage. The earbuds’ tuning is what it is; accessories improve fit, comfort, and durability, not audio fidelity.

### Final Thoughts

The BESNOOW earbuds are capable on their own, but a few well-chosen extras — foam tips, a wireless charging pad, and a cleaning kit — will make them feel more premium than their price suggests. Start with the tips; that’s the highest-impact change you can make for under a few dollars.
