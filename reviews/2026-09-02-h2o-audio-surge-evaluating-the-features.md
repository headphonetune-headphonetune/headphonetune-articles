For everyday listening, the sound isolation and noise control situation The specs list "noise control: Sound Isolation" and the model name includes "Noise-Cancelling-Attribute." That's misleading. True noise cancellation uses microphones and active electronics to generate anti-noise waves. Sound isolation is passive - it just blocks sound by sealing your ear canal. These aren't noise-cancelling headphones. They're isolating earbuds. The distinction matters because [active noise cancellation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Active_noise_control) works across all frequencies, while isolation primarily attenuates higher frequencies and does little for deep, rumbling noises like airplane engines or subway rumble.
