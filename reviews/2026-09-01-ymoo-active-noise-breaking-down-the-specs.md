### Frequently Asked Questions About the YMOO Active Noise

The built-in 850mAh battery provides up to 100 hours of playback on a full charge. A 10-minute charge adds 5 hours of power, and a full charge takes about 3 hours.

### Can I use these headphones with an airplane's in-flight entertainment system?
Yes. The headphones include a 3.5mm audio cable and an airplane adapter, so you can plug directly into the seat's audio jack for wired listening.

 We took a close look at what the YMOO Active Noise offers and how it compares to other options in its class.### What noise cancellation features do these headphones have?

Yes. The companion app offers 6 EQ presets for customizing the audio. The headphones also support Hi-Res Audio and LDAC, with 40mm dynamic drivers and a frequency response up to 40kHz.
