A sleep mask with built-in headphones solves one specific problem: how to listen to something as you fall asleep without earbuds digging into your ears or a cable strangling you at 3 a.m. The MUSICOZY GH01 approaches that drawback with a [Bluetooth 5.4](https://www.bluetooth.com/about-us/) module, a 200mAh battery rated for 14 hours, and a blackout mask design that claims to accommodate side sleepers. Those three things are the core of what you're buying, so it's worth looking at each one closely - including where the design pushes back. [Bluetooth 5.4](https://www.bluetooth.com/about-us/) and the audio side [Bluetooth 5.4](https://www.bluetooth.com/about-us/) is genuinely current. It brings better power efficiency and more stable connections than the 5.0 and 5.1 chips still common in economical sleep masks. In practice. That means fewer dropouts when you roll over and a connection that holds up if your phone is on the nightstand a few feet away. For everyday listening. The stated range is 33 feet, which is standard for Class 2 Bluetooth and more than enough for a bedroom. The impedance is 32 ohm with dynamic drivers - that's a normal, easy-to-drive spec. Your phone will power these without issue. A built-in microphone means you can take a call without pulling the mask off, though you'll sound muffled to whoever's on the other end since the mic sits against fabric near your face. That's less fussy in the dark, but it also means you need to remember where the buttons are by feel. In real-world use. The honest limitation here's that "HD stereo sound" is relative. Battery life and charging The 200mAh battery is rated for over 14 hours of continuous playtime. That comfortably covers a full night's sleep for most people. Which is the whole point - you don't want a low-battery beep waking you at 4 a.m. The charging port is [USB-C](https://www.usb.org/usb-type-c). Which is the right call in 2025. No hunting for a micro-USB cable. The trade-off is that the 14 hours is a single-night figure. Not a multi-night one. If you use these for both sleep and daytime listening, you'll be charging most days. There's no fast-charge spec listed, so assume a standard charge time of a couple of hours. The battery is also not user-replaceable as far as the data shows. Which means the mask's useful life is tied to the battery's lifespan. That's typical for this category but worth knowing going in. ### Frequently Asked Questions About the MUSICOZY 3D Sleep
### Will this mask actually stay on and stay comfortable if I sleep on my side?
The mask is built with a 3D ergonomic shape and a soft nose cushion, and the adjustable Velcro strap is designed to keep it in place for side sleepers. The wider, deeper eye space is meant to avoid pressure on your eyes and face. That said, the earpieces sit on your ear rather than inside it, so how it feels against a pillow will depend on your head shape.

### How long does the battery last. And how do I charge it?
MUSICOZY rates it at over 14 hours of continuous playtime from a 200mAh battery, which should cover a full night. It charges over USB Type-C. And the listing notes there are no beeping low-battery alerts to wake you.

### Can I wash it, and does it survive sweat or rain?
The electronic components are removable. So the mask itself is fully washable. It isn't water resistant. So keep it away from heavy sweat, rain, or full submersion.

### Does it block out noise as well as light?
You will find no active noise cancellation. So it will not silence a snoring partner or traffic on its own. The mask blocks light with its blackout design. And two pairs of earplugs are included if you want extra noise blocking.

### What is included. And what is the warranty?
The box contains the sleep mask. A charging cable, a user manual, a carry bag, and two pairs of earplugs. MUSICOZY offers a 12-month warranty. A 60-day refund without returning the item, and a 90-day free replacement for quality issues.
