The 3.5mm plug is compatible with most audio and video devices that have a standard headphone jack, like computers, tablets, or media players. The cord is 5'6" long and the plug is angled to reduce accidental pull-out.

The earcups are made of heavy-duty ABS plastic to resist breakage, and the cord has a reinforced strain connection at the headphone end. The cable is not retractable despite the listing mentioning that feature, so treat it as a standard straight cord.
