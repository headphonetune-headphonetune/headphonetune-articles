The most important spec here is the connectivity. A single 3.5mm jack with no USB dongle means this headset works with anything that has a headphone port. You are not locked into a proprietary wireless dongle or a specific console ecosystem. The trade-off is that you get no virtual surround sound processing on-board. The listing mentions compatibility with Windows Sonic spatial sound on Windows 10 and Xbox One, but that's a software feature handled by the console or PC, not the headset itself. The headset just receives the stereo signal; the spatial processing happens elsewhere. So, if you were hoping for built-in 7.1 surround, this isn't that.
