### Frequently Asked Questions About the Bywulanda Kids Headphones
### Will these headphones work with my child's tablet or phone?
They connect through a USB-C headphone jack, so they work with laptops, desktops, tablets, cellphones, and other devices that have that port. They won't work with devices that only have a USB-C charging port or a standard 3.5mm headphone jack.

### How does the volume limiting work?
There's a switch that lets you cap the volume at either 85dB or 94dB. The 85dB setting is for everyday listening - and the 94dB setting is meant for noisy places like planes or subways where your child needs more volume to hear clearly.

### Can my child use these for online classes and video calls?
Yes. There's a built-in microphone, so they work for online courses, voice calls, and video chats. The 40mm drivers handle audio for studying, music, movies, and games as well.

### Do the LED lights stay on all the time?
No, the lightning bolt LEDs only turn on when the headphones are plugged in - and there's a button to turn the lights on or off. Your child can keep them lit or switch them off depending on what they prefer.

### How do these fit and hold up for kids?
The headband adjusts to fit heads from kids to teenagers, and the earcups rotate at multiple angles. They fold up for travel, weigh 5.3 ounces, and come with an 18-month warranty.
