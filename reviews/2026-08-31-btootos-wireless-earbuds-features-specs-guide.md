The listing uses "ENC Noise Reduction" and "[noise cancelling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Active_noise_control)" language. ENC (Environmental Noise Cancellation) is for the microphones during calls. It filters out background noise so the person on the other end can hear you better. That is not the same as [Active Noise Cancellation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Active_noise_control) ([ANC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Active_noise_control)). Which uses microphones to generate anti-noise to cancel out ambient sound for your listening. The technical specs confirm this: noise control is listed as "Passive Noise Cancellation, Sound Isolation." That means the only noise isolation you get is from the physical seal of the silicone tips. On a noisy subway or in a bustling office. You'll still hear the rumble and chatter. The ENC will help your voice come through clearly on calls, but it won't silence the world around you. If you're expecting [ANC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Active_noise_control) performance like you'd get from premium earbuds. You'll be disappointed. If you just want to hear your music without the outside world being completely muted, the passive isolation from a capable seal will do the job.
