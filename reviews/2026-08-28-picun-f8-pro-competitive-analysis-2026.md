### Frequently Asked Questions About the Picun F8 Pro
### How long does the battery last with and without noise cancelling enabled?
The headphones offer up to 140 hours of playtime with ANC off, and about 60+ hours with ANC on. A full recharge via USB-C takes roughly 1-2 hours.

### Can I use these headphones with a wired connection?
Yes. They include a 3.5mm audio cable and a 3.5mm jack, so you can use them wired when the battery runs out or with devices that lack Bluetooth.

### What noise cancelling modes are available?
You getthree modes: Deep. Mild, and Transparency. Deep is for strong noise reduction (up to -52dB), Mild for lighter filtering, and Transparency lets ambient sound in.

### Can I connect to multiple devices at once?
Yes, multipoint Bluetooth support lets you switch between a phone, tablet, laptop, or PC without re-pairing each time.

### Do the headphones have a built-in microphone for calls?
Yes, dual ENC microphones are built in, designed to keep your voice clear during calls even in noisy environments.
