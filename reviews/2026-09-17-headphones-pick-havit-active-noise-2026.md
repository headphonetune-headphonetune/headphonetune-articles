The battery is rated for up to 50 hours of playtime. And a full charge takes about 2.5 hours via the included Type-C cable.

Yes. They support both Bluetooth and a 3.5mm AUX cable, and the cable is detachable, so you can plug in directly when you don't want to use wireless.
