### Frequently Asked Questions About the Boean Bluetooth Headphones
### How long does the battery last on a full charge, and how long does it take to recharge?
The headphones give you up to 16 hours of playtime per charge. Recharging takes about 2 hours using the supplied USB-C cable; other cables aren't recommended.

### Can I use these headphones while running in the rain or with heavy sweat?
Yes, they've an IPX7 waterproof rating, so they can handle rain, sweat, and moisture during workouts. They are not recommended for swimming.

### Will these earbuds connect to my smartwatch?
No. They pair with iOS, Android, and Windows devices via Bluetooth 5.3, but smartwatch connectivity isn't supported.

### What makes these suitable for sports use?
They include flexible ear hooks, in-ear tips, and a headband for a secure fit, plus they weigh only 18.5 grams. The design is meant to stay in place during running, cycling, gym workouts, and similar activities.

### Do these headphones have a microphone for phone calls?
Yes, a microphone is included, and the listing mentions clear calls technology for crystal-clear phone conversations.
