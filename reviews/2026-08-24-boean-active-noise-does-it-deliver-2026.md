### Frequently Asked Questions About the Boean Active Noise
### How long does the battery last on a single charge?
The headphones deliver up to 120 hours of playback on a full charge. And the battery takes about 2 hours to recharge.

### Can I use these headphones in wired mode if the battery dies?
Yes. There's a 3.5mm AUX wired mode for uninterrupted audio when power is low, but note that active noise cancellation (ANC) doesn't work in wired mode.

### What devices are these headphones compatible with?
They work with cellphones. Computers, laptops, tablets, and gaming consoles via Bluetooth 6.0, with a wireless range of 33 feet.

### How effective is the noise cancellation?
Hybrid ANC blocks up to 96.7% of background noise. Reducing ambient noise by 45dB. There is also a Transparency Mode to hear surroundings without removing the headphones.

### Are these headphones suitable for calls in noisy environments?
Yes, they've 5 ENC microphones that filter out traffic and conversations to capture your voice clearly, making them good for online meetings or calls in loud places.
