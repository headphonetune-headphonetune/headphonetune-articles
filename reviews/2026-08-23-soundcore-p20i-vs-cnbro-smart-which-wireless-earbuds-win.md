# Soundcore P20i vs CNBRO Smart: Which Wireless Earbuds Win?

> Originally published on [www.headphonetune.com](https://www.headphonetune.com/review/wireless-earbuds-q20i-vs-cnbro-wireles-smart-lab-tested-2026/)

### Two Budget Earbuds, One Clear Winner

Picking between budget wireless earbuds usually means deciding which corners you can live with. The Soundcore P20i True and the CNBRO Wireles Smart both target the same buyer, but they take very different approaches to getting there. After spending time with both, the gap between them is wider than the price difference suggests.

### Sound and Battery: The Practical Differences

The Soundcore P20i True delivers a warm, bass-forward sound signature that works well for pop, hip-hop, and podcasts. You get punchy lows without the mids feeling hollow, which is rare at this level. The CNBRO Wireles Smart leans brighter and thinner, with vocals that sit forward but percussion that can get harsh on louder tracks. If you mostly listen to spoken word or acoustic music, the CNBRO is acceptable. For anything with rhythm, the P20i is clearly more enjoyable.

Battery life tells a similar story. The Soundcore P20i True gives you around 10 hours per charge, with the case adding another 30 or so. The CNBRO Wireles Smart manages roughly 6 hours per charge, and its case tops out around 18 total. That difference matters if you commute, travel, or simply forget to charge things regularly. A full week of daily use on the P20i is realistic; the CNBRO will have you hunting for a charger by Wednesday.

### Fit, Controls, and Everyday Annoyances

Both earbuds are small and light, but the P20i True has a more stable fit thanks to its angled nozzle and included ear tips in multiple sizes. The CNBRO relies on a one-size-fits-all tip that works for some ears but slips out during workouts or long walks. Touch controls on the Soundcore respond consistently; the CNBRO sometimes ignores taps or registers double presses when you meant a single one. These are small things individually, but they add up over a month of use.

One area where the CNBRO Wireles Smart actually wins is transparency mode. It lets in more ambient sound naturally, which helps if you need to hear traffic or coworkers. The P20i True has a transparency setting too, but it sounds slightly muffled, like you are listening through a thin wall. For indoor use, the CNBRO is less isolating, which some people prefer.

### Connectivity and Call Quality

Bluetooth pairing is straightforward on both. The Soundcore P20i True uses Bluetooth 5.3, so it holds a stable connection even when your phone is in a backpack or across the room. The CNBRO uses Bluetooth 5.0, which is fine for most situations but drops occasionally if you move between rooms. Calls on the P20i are clearer for the person on the other end; the CNBRO picks up background noise more aggressively, making you sound distant in busy environments.

If you plan to use these for work calls or video meetings, the Soundcore is the safer choice. The CNBRO works in a quiet room, but that is about it.

### Our Verdict

For most people, the Soundcore P20i True is the better buy. It lasts longer, sounds fuller, fits more securely, and handles calls more reliably. The CNBRO Wireles Smart only makes sense if you specifically want stronger ambient awareness or you find the P20i uncomfortable in your ears. If you are weighing these two against each other, the choice is clear.
