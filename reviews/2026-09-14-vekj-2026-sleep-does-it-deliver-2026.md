### How long do these earbuds actually last on a charge?
You get 6 to 8 hours from a single charge, and the charging case extends that to 48 hours total. A full charge takes about 1.5 to 2 hours via USB-C.

### Are they comfortable enough for side sleeping?
Each earbud weighs 3.0 grams and the contoured shape is designed to sit without pressure, which is the main point of the mini design. There's also a sleep mode that silences low-battery and power-off notification sounds so they don't wake you.

### What can I control from the charging case screen?
The full-color LED touch screen handles music playback. Track changes, calls, volume, and voice assistant, plus sleep mode, timer, wallpaper, brightness, and language switching. It also shows battery levels for both the earbuds and the case.

### Can I wear them in the rain or while sweating?
Yes. They carry an IPX7 waterproof rating, which covers sweat, rain, and dust. That's a full submersion rating, so splashes and workouts aren't a concern.

### Do they work with my phone and other devices?
They use Bluetooth 5.4 with a 33-foot range and work with most Bluetooth-enabled devices across iOS, Android, and Windows. There are also five EQ modes (standard, classic, jazz, rock, pop) if you want to adjust the sound.
