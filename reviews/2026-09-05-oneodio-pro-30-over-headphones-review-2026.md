### Can I use these headphones with both my phone and a mixer without needing an adapter?
Yes, the headphones come with both a standard 6.35mm plug and a 3.5mm plug, and both are detachable. This means you can connect directly to devices with either jack size. Like a phone (3.5mm) or a mixer (6.35mm), without a separate adapter.

The ear cushions are made of protein leather with memory foam - and the headband is adjustable and stretchable. The ear cups also swivel 90 degrees for single-ear monitoring, which helps reduce fatigue during extended mixing or mastering work.
