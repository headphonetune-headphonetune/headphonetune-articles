Yes, the Connect Air supports dual headphone connection, allowing up to two AirPods or other Bluetooth headphones to stream audio simultaneously. Battery life drops to 15 hours with two connected, versus 20 hours with one.

It supports aptX Low Latency, which delivers ultra-low audio delay, but only when your headphones also support aptX Low Latency. With standard SBC headphones, latency will be higher.

Yes, the built-in battery can be used while charging, so you can keep it plugged into a power source during long flights or sessions without losing audio.

Any device with a standard 3.5mm headphone jack, including in-flight entertainment systems, TVs, computers, Nintendo Switch, Xbox/PlayStation controllers, gym equipment, A/V receivers, and portable DVD players.
