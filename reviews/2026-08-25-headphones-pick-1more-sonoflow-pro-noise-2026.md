The 40mm dynamic drivers with a frequency response up to 40kHz are paired with [LDAC](https://www.sony.com/electronics/sound-quality/ldac) support. [LDAC](https://www.sony.com/electronics/sound-quality/ldac) is a big deal because it transmits roughly three times the data of standard SBC Bluetooth codecs. In practice, that means if you're streaming from a service like Tidal or Amazon Music Ultra HD, you'll hear finer detail in cymbals, string textures, and vocal breaths compared to AAC or SBC. The "dual Hi-Res gold certification" is marketing shorthand, but the [LDAC](https://www.sony.com/electronics/sound-quality/ldac) support is real and functional.
